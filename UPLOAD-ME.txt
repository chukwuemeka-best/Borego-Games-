CITY SHOWDOWN - how to put it in your Borego-Games repository

1. Open your Borego-Games repository on GitHub.
2. Click "Add file" > "Upload files". 
3. Drag in the FOLDER called city-showdown (from this zip). GitHub keeps the folder name.
   (It has just two files: index.html and cover.png.)
4. Click "Commit changes". Wait 1-2 minutes.

Check it opens: https://chukwuemeka-best.github.io/Borego-Games/city-showdown/
Check the picture:  https://chukwuemeka-best.github.io/Borego-Games/city-showdown/cover.png

In your Borego admin panel, add the game:
  Name:        City Showdown
  Link:        https://chukwuemeka-best.github.io/Borego-Games/city-showdown/
  Picture link: https://chukwuemeka-best.github.io/Borego-Games/city-showdown/cover.png   (no slash at the end)
  Players:     1 to 4
  Solo allowed: yes

The emoji, live chat and reactions come from Borego's game panel automatically - nothing is built into this game.
